Hi there! 👋 I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at City University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Ray C. C. Cheung. I previously studied computer science at the National University of Singapore and Xi’an Jiaotong University.你好！👋 我在香港城市大学攻读电子工程博士学位，目前是博士三年级，导师为 Ray C. C. Cheung 教授。此前，我曾在新加坡国立大学和西安交通大学学习计算机科学。

My research interests lie in multimodal recommendation and AI infrastructure. I study how to recommend items with limited interaction history, how to handle conflicting signals across modalities, and how to recover KV caches during large language model inference.我的研究方向是多模态推荐与 AI 基础设施，关注交互历史有限的物品推荐、多模态信号冲突，以及大语言模型推理中的 KV 缓存恢复。
